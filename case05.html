Overview

Creating a high-converting shopping experience for a premium DTC brand.

The eCommerce experience suffered from fragmented navigation, inconsistent UI patterns, and friction throughout the shopping journey. While traffic remained strong, customers struggled to discover products, evaluate options confidently, and complete purchases.

My Role UX/UI Designer
Domain Direct-to-Consumer eCommerce
Timeline ~8 Weeks
Team Design / Product / Growth
Search and discovery interface supporting higher user intent and conversion.
The Problem

Traffic was strong. Conversion was weak.

Users visited the store and explored products, but friction throughout the shopping journey prevented them from completing purchases.

↘

Low Conversion Rate

Visitors browsed but hesitated to purchase.

◎

Product Discovery Friction

Users struggled to find the right products quickly.

⚠

Checkout Abandonment

Complex purchase flows created unnecessary drop-offs.

Growth Strategy

Reducing friction across the customer journey.

Pillar 01

Improve Product Discovery

Reorganized navigation, collection structures, and filtering systems to help customers find products faster.

Pillar 02

Increase Purchase Confidence

Redesigned PDPs to surface product benefits, reviews, sizing information, and social proof at key decision moments.

Final Solution

A premium shopping experience designed for clarity, confidence, and conversion.

The redesigned experience helps customers discover products effortlessly, understand value quickly, and complete purchases with minimal friction.

Solution 01
Reimagined Homepage Experience

Clear value proposition, stronger product storytelling, and optimized merchandising hierarchy.

Solution 02
Enhanced Product Listing Pages

Smart filtering, improved sorting, and collection experiences designed around shopping intent.

Solution 03
Conversion-Focused Product Detail Pages

Clear product information, trust signals, and cross-sell opportunities designed to increase purchase confidence.

Solution 04
Optimized Cart & Checkout

Streamlined checkout flow with reduced friction and a mobile-first experience to improve conversions.

Measurable business outcomes.
